{% include components/project_banner.html %}

<div lang="en" markdown="1">

Wunduniik is a Skyrim Anniversary Edition modlist of about 3,800 mods that reworks the whole game: graphics, combat, quests, cities and the interface.

## About This Modlist

It is the heaviest list on the site. The Mod Contents page shows every section: about 730 texture and mesh mods, 350 for combat and animations, 290 for gameplay, 180 for cities and towns, and more than 100 quest mods.

### Key Features

- **Graphics**: ENB lighting with Lux and Lux Via, Cathedral 3D grass and plants, and 2K textures across the game.
- **Combat and animations**: About 350 mods for combat moves, animations and enemy behaviour.
- **New places and quests**: About 180 mods for cities, towns and villages, 170 for new or reworked locations, and more than 100 quest mods.
- **Interface**: About 130 interface mods: menus, item and spell tooltips, compass and spell cards.
